A little about me

I've always been a wonderer, especially when it comes to people. Why do they behave the way they do? What emotions, habits, and experiences shape their actions and decisions? Observing people, analysing their behaviours, and understanding the psychology behind their choices genuinely excites me. It's this fascination that draws me towards the intersection of behavioural science and product design. Originally from Goa, I'm currently a final-year Communication Design student at Symbiosis Institute of Design, Nagpur.

Beyond the screen

When words aren't enough, I paint. Nature walks and bird-watching make me pause and pay attention. Writing is my little world: thoughts, emotions, ideas, stories, and getting lost in a good book.
