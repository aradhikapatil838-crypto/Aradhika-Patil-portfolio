UX Research · Usability Testing · Redesign

Making YONO SBI Easier for Different Kinds of Users

Redesigning core banking experiences around different levels of familiarity, confidence and everyday banking needs.

My Role

UX Research · Usability Testing · UI/UX Design

Team

Aradhika Patil & Khushboo

Methods

Moderated Testing · Think Aloud · 5-Second Test · Card Sorting · A/B Testing

Participants

6 users

Testing YONO SBI with six different users.

We tested the YONO SBI app with 6 users to understand how familiarity, banking experience and digital confidence shaped their experience. The study focused on checking balances and transactions, sending money, and adding a payee.

Who we tested with

Regular YONO user

Familiar with the app and everyday banking tasks.

SBI user, new to YONO

Familiar with SBI banking, but new to its mobile app.

First-time YONO user

Exploring the app’s navigation and banking flows for the first time.

Young digital user

Used to quick UPI experiences and other digital payment apps.

Occasional user

Uses the app infrequently and relies on visible, recognisable actions.

Senior user

Rarely uses YONO independently and needs clearer guidance.

Pain Points

01

Different users, different needs

Regular users wanted transactions and bill payments, while younger users wanted budgeting and spending alerts.

02

No personalization

The same experience was shown to users with different needs and priorities.

03

Frequent actions were harder to reach

Users’ most-used actions were not always easily accessible.

04

Need for budgeting

Users wanted an easier way to track spending and manage their budget.

05

Low error prevention

Unclear transaction status, especially during network issues, created fear of failed or duplicate payments.

What Did We Learn?

Poor Discoverability

Core actions were available, but users often had to scan, search and explore to find them.

Confusing Navigation

Similar transfer options and unclear labels created hesitation and incorrect paths.

Long & Fragmented Flows

As tasks became more complex, completion dropped and errors increased.

Low Error Prevention

The interface often informed users after an error instead of helping prevent it.

Different Users Needed Different Support

Experienced users moved faster, while first-time, occasional and senior users needed greater visibility and guidance.

User Needs

01 — Easy Access to Frequent Actions

Users need their most-used actions visible and easy to reach.

02 — Simplicity & Relevance

Users need to see what matters to them, without being overwhelmed by unnecessary options.

03 — Better Money Management

3 out of 6 users mentioned that a budgeting feature would help them manage their money better.

04 — Error Prevention & Reassurance

Users need clear guidance before errors and confirmation after transactions to feel confident about their actions.

Solution

01 — Tag Important Actions

Users can add tags like Favourite to actions they want quick access to.

02 — A Personalized Home

The home screen shows only what is relevant to the user, reducing unnecessary clutter.

03 — Frequently Used Tasks

Common actions automatically appear on the home screen for faster access.

04 — Budgeting & Spending

A budgeting feature helps users track spending and manage their money better.
